Privacy policy

This policy covers Imran Cash Carry POS, the point-of-sale software of Imran Cash & Carry, Main Chowk Cand-a-qila, Near Green Pharmacy, Pakistan.

What the software does with Google data

  • It asks for one Google permission only: drive.file. With it the software can see and change only the files and folders it has created itself in the shop's Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in that Drive.
  • It uses that folder to keep copies of the shop's own records (sales, purchases, stock, customer accounts) so that the shop's counters stay in step and the data survives a computer failure.
  • It does not read the user's e-mail, contacts, photos or any other Google data.

Sharing

The data stays in the shop's own Google Drive and on the shop's own computers. It is not sold, shared with or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Removing access

The shop can remove the software's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the folder "Imran Cash&Carry POS" from its Google Drive.
