Imran Cash Carry POS

Imran Cash Carry POS is the point-of-sale software used at the counters of Imran Cash & Carry, Bhalokay Main Road, Pakistan. It records sales, purchases, stock and customer accounts on the shop's own computers.

The shop signs in once with its own Google account so that every counter can keep a copy of the shop's data in a folder of that account's Google Drive. This keeps the counters in step with each other and keeps the data safe if a computer fails.

The software is for the shop's own staff only. It is not offered to the public.
